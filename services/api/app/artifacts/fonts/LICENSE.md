# Bundled fonts

`DejaVuSans.ttf` and `DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf` are part of the **DejaVu Fonts**
project, a permissively licensed superset of the Bitstream Vera fonts. They are
redistributable under the Bitstream Vera / DejaVu license (free to use, bundle,
and redistribute, including in commercial products; the copyright notice must be
retained and the fonts may not be sold on their own).

They are bundled here so PDF renders reproduce full Turkish orthography
(ş/ğ/ı/İ/ç/ö/ü and typographic punctuation) deterministically and identically on
Windows, Linux CI, and the cloud runtime, without depending on any font
installed on the host.
